# evaOS Mac Access v0.1 private RC

This candidate is for private install, forward-update, and uninstall evaluation only.

The attached evidence may prove an exact Developer ID signed, notarized, stapled Mac Access artifact and signed private update feed. It does not prove pristine-Mac onboarding, live broker-selected VM-to-Mac Computer Use, Workbench coexistence, customer readiness, public distribution, or rollout authorization.

Rollback authorization and rollback execution are not proven. They remain blocked on the broker-owned signer and staging-key contract tracked in [evaos-ws-proxy #76](https://github.com/electricsheephq/evaos-ws-proxy/issues/76).
